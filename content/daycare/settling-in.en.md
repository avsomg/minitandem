# Settling in

Settling-in means the first days or weeks at a crèche: short visits that grow longer, often with a parent there at first. Several crèches make it compulsory before regular attendance. Plan time off work for it, and use the questions at the end when you visit a crèche.

## How long it takes

- **Normally two weeks:** pop e poppa (Romont), KidsCare (Villars-sur-Glâne) and the Crèche de Gibloux, which adjusts it if needed.
- **Mandarine (Fribourg):** at least 3 weeks.
- **Kita Zauberschlössli (Tafers):** 2 to 4 weeks, with a parent present.
- **Kita Konfetti (Muntelier):** usually about 2 to 3 weeks, following the Berlin model: parents are present at first, and the time the child spends without them grows step by step.
- **Family day care:** famiya arranges a settling-in with gradual hours when the child's wellbeing calls for it, in principle for no more than 3 weeks in a row.

## What the steps look like

- **Les Lucioles (Marly):** 4 visits over 2 weeks: about an hour with a parent, an hour alone, a half-day with a meal, then a short day with the nap.
- **Les Canetons (Estavayer-le-Lac):** the commune's page describes 2 consecutive weeks: an entry meeting with the group's reference person, a first visit of 1.5 to 2 hours, a half-day with lunch, then a full day with lunch and nap. The crèche's own concept ends with a short day instead, and says the pace and the number of sessions can be adapted to the child.
- **Les Chemins de l'enfance (Riaz):** in principle 5 visits of increasing length, counting the interview, within at most one month. Settling-in can be extended to 8 visits.

## Plan your own time

- At pop e poppa (Romont), a parent or another close adult must stay reachable and available throughout settling-in.
- Les Chemins de l'enfance (Riaz) asks parents to stay available to collect the child at any time.

## What it costs during settling-in

**The normal fee from day one**

- KidsCare (Villars-sur-Glâne) and Little Green House (Bulle) charge the normal fee from the first day of settling-in.

**A share of the fee**

- **City of Fribourg, subsidised crèches, for families living in the city:** 30% of the contract invoice during settling-in, for at most one month; after that the full amount is due.
- **La Providence (Fribourg):** fees are reduced by 70%, so the family pays 30%.
- **Kita Zauberschlössli (Tafers):** 50% of the calculated rate until the child attends a full day, under rules in force since 1 March 2024.
- **Kita Seisemüsli (Plaffeien):** 60% of the normal tariff.
- **Les Chemins de l'enfance (Riaz):** 40% of the daily rate for each settling-in visit.

**A flat amount**

- **Brin d'Eveil Montessori (Châtel-Saint-Denis):** CHF 450 for the two weeks, under rules in force since 1 January 2022; it is not refunded if the family withdraws.
- **Les Fripouilles (Corminboeuf):** CHF 150 per child for settling-in.

**Only the hours used**

- **famiya (family day care):** only the hours actually used are billed.

## What to ask the crèche

- How many visits are planned, how long is each one, and when does the parent leave the room?
- Who decides to shorten or extend settling-in, and what happens to the date regular attendance starts?
- What does settling-in cost, and from which day is the full fee due?
- How quickly must a parent be able to come and collect the child during the first weeks?
- Is there a trial period afterwards, and what notice applies on each side?
- Who is the child's reference person, and how will the team keep you informed?

If settling-in does not go well, see the guide "When a crèche cannot take your child, or settling in is hard".

Rules and prices change: ask your commune or the crèche.
