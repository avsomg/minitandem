# When a crèche cannot take your child, or settling in is hard

This guide is for when a crèche cannot take your child or ends the contract, or the first weeks do not go well. Start by talking with the crèche; then see which of the services below fits. If a crèche ends the contract, see "When no place is free" in the guide "Getting a daycare place in canton Fribourg". This guide gives no medical advice.

## Talk with the crèche

Ask for a meeting with the team. Ask what they observe, whether a slower or longer settling-in is possible, and whether outside support could help.

- Some crèches say settling-in can be extended: pop e poppa, and Les Libellules (Marly), which can add a week if the team sees the need.
- The canton can subsidise the extra care a crèche gives a child who needs particular care, for example because of an illness or a disability.
- The crèche files the request and shows the extra cost compared with ordinary care. Ask whether your crèche can apply.
- For the 2024–2025 school year, 62 such requests were filed, 36 of them for crèches.
- The early-education service SEI also advises crèches and other early-childhood providers on caring for children with particular needs. According to the canton, only a few providers have had this support so far.

## What crèches' rules say

Some crèches set a trial period, or a short period after settling-in, during which the contract or the registration can be ended. The rules differ:

- **La Providence (Fribourg):** a trial period of 3 months from the start. During it the crèche may end the contract if the child has difficulties the team cannot handle in a group.
- **Brin d'Eveil Montessori (Châtel-Saint-Denis):** 2 months, settling-in included. The crèche can end the placement at once if the child does not integrate well; parents can withdraw the child at once.
- **Crèche de Gibloux:** after settling-in, parents or the crèche can withdraw the registration within 10 working days.
- **Les Lucioles (Marly):** after settling-in, parents or the crèche can cancel the registration within 10 days.
- **famiya (family day care):** the first month after settling-in is a trial period. In an open-ended contract, either side can end it in writing with 7 calendar days' notice. Ordinary early termination of a fixed-term contract is for famiya only; either side can still end any contract at once for just cause.

## Early education at home: the SEI

- The SEI is free for families: the canton pays.
- The Service éducatif itinérant (SEI) offers early education from birth until the second year of school. It is for children whose development is unusual, different or at risk, and for children with a disability.
- Its sessions take place where the child lives, in close collaboration with the parents.
- Parents can contact the SEI themselves, without commitment, by phone, by letter or through its website, [sei-fribourg.ch](https://www.sei-fribourg.ch/). A paediatrician or another professional can also refer a child, with the parents' agreement.
- A first overall assessment estimates whether the child's development matches that of children of the same age.

## Speech therapy and psychomotricity

- Speech therapy is for children with difficulties in language, speech, communication or oral function, and for their parents.
- Psychomotricity is for children with serious difficulties in how perception, thinking and behaviour work together, and how this shows in their body.
- For a child aged 0 to 4, parents themselves contact a speech therapist or a psychomotor therapist who holds a cantonal approval.
- If support is recommended, the speech therapist files a request with the canton's special-education service (SESAM), and the family receives a decision on it.
- The canton lists the SEI as a recognised provider of psychomotricity for children below school age.
- Both kinds of support are, in principle, for children from birth to the end of the first school year (1H).

## Parent counselling in your district

Specialist nurses for early childhood advise and support parents of children up to age 4. Find your district:

- Broye, Gruyère and the Sarine countryside: [Croix-Rouge fribourgeoise](https://www.croix-rouge-fr.ch/fr/la-pour-vous/familles/consultations-parents-enfants)
- Greater Fribourg (Fribourg city, Marly, Villars-sur-Glâne, Granges-Paccot, Givisiez): [Office Familial](https://www.officefamilial.ch/fr/services/puericulture/notre-offre)
- Veveyse: [RSSV](https://rssv.ch/puericulture)
- Glâne: [Réseau Santé Glâne](https://www.sante-glane.ch/puericulture)
- Sense: [senseera](https://www.senseera.ch/muetterundvaeterberatung/)
- Lac: [RSL GNS](https://www.rsl-gns.ch/de/muetter-vaeterberatung/)

## Advice and urgent help: Permanence Intake

The canton's youth office (SEJ) runs the [Permanence Intake](https://www.fr.ch/vie-quotidienne/en-cas-de-difficultes/tout-sur-la-protection-des-enfants-et-des-jeunes/permanence-et-secteur-intake), which gives parents information and advice and offers emergency help.
