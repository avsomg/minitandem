Little Owls Playgroup

A parent-run playgroup for children aged 2 to 4 years. A parent stays with the child for the whole session.

We meet every Tuesday from 9:00 to 11:00 in the community hall, Training Street 1, 9999 Examplestadt.

Registration is not needed. Just come along.

Contribution: CHF 5 per family per visit.

The playgroup is closed during school holidays.
