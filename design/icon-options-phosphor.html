Minitandem · Phosphor icons for the category tile, five variants

Phosphor Icons 2.1.1 (npm “@phosphor-icons/core”, MIT), drawn in the category inks and tints already in the app. The same eight real rows as before (English, from the app on 14 October 2026), at phone width. Every icon stays next to its written category, so colour and drawing are never the only cue. In every variant the icon or tile is centred vertically on the row, also next to two-line names (for example “Aux Merveilles d’Alice · café with play area”).

Recommendation: A, Phosphor duotone on the current tile. Phosphor has a real picture for every category (a swimmer, a horse, a runner, a coffee cup, music notes, a tree), so the tile can do what the owner wanted: say the category at a glance. The duotone layer adds a little depth inside each drawing without a new colour, all ten pictures share one stroke and one style, and the change is only the icon files. C (regular) is the calm fallback if A feels too busy at 24 rows. B makes the list heavier, D adds colour without adding meaning, and E (B with the coloured word) is the best no-tile option but its solid silhouettes weigh more than A’s outlines.

Variant A · recommended

Duotone on the tile

Phosphor duotone, 24 px, on the current 40 px tint tile, in the category ink. The second layer is Phosphor’s own: the same ink at 20 % opacity, which on the tint becomes a slightly deeper tint inside the drawing.
